# FAQ

## How long does PPF installation take?

Front protection typically takes one to two days. Full-body installation usually takes three to five days depending on the vehicle and paint condition.

## Can PPF be removed safely?

Yes. Correctly installed premium film can be removed without damaging factory paint.

## Do I need paint correction before PPF?

Only if the paint has visible defects that would remain underneath the film. We inspect the surface first and recommend correction only when necessary.

## How is the final price calculated?

The final estimate depends on vehicle size, body complexity, selected protection zones, paint condition and additional services.

## Process

5. Контроль качества — Проверяем кромки, качество отделки, точность установки и прозрачность поверхности перед выдачей.
