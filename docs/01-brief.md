# 01 — Product & Brand Brief

## Project

**NOIR Detailing**

Fictional premium automotive detailing studio in Moscow.

## Purpose

Create a commercial-quality website that:

1. Generates qualified detailing inquiries.
2. Communicates premium positioning.
3. Demonstrates craftsmanship and technical expertise.
4. Works as a strong portfolio case for web development services.

## Positioning

NOIR is not positioned as a car wash or general auto service.

It is positioned as a **premium automotive care atelier** focused on protection, preservation, and visual perfection.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- owners of premium and performance cars;
- age roughly 25–50;
- values vehicle condition, resale value, appearance, and quality of materials;
- willing to pay for expert service rather than choose the cheapest option.

Typical vehicles:

- Porsche
- BMW M
- Mercedes-AMG
- Range Rover
- Audi RS
- Lexus
- Tesla
- Ferrari
- Bentley

## Main Services

- Paint Protection Film
- Ceramic Coating
- Paint Correction
- Interior Detailing
- Premium Maintenance Wash
- Window Tint
- Dechroming

## Main User Goal

**Request a personalized estimate.**

## Secondary Goals

- Browse completed projects.
- Understand services and pricing.
- Verify quality and trust signals.
- Contact the studio directly.

## Brand Character

- precise
- premium
- calm
- technical
- editorial
- cinematic
- confident
- tactile

## Brand Promise

Protect and preserve exceptional vehicles with meticulous workmanship and premium materials.

## Core Message

> Protection without compromise.

Alternative supporting line:

> Premium paint protection, correction and detailing for exceptional automobiles.

## Value Proposition

NOIR combines:

- certified materials;
- highly controlled installation process;
- expert preparation;
- transparent packages;
- portfolio-backed quality;
- long-term protection.

## Trust Signals

Use concrete signals instead of generic claims:

- 210+ cars protected
- 10-year PPF warranty
- 4.9 / 5 client rating
- certified installation
- controlled lighting studio
- premium film brands
- multi-stage quality control

## Primary CTA

**Request assessment**

Russian version:

**Получить расчёт**

## Secondary CTA

**Explore our work**

Russian version:

**Смотреть проекты**

## Tone of Voice

Short, precise, calm.

Avoid:

- shouting;
- hype;
- excessive promises;
- generic phrases such as «индивидуальный подход» and «команда профессионалов».

Prefer:

- short claims;
- concrete numbers;
- technical language;
- simple explanations.

## Do Not

- neon styling;
- aggressive tuning aesthetics;
- gaming UI;
- fake luxury;
- black/gold clichés;
- huge walls of text;
- stock supercars with no connection to detailing;
- excessive animation.
