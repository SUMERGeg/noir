## Page Goals

### `/`

Primary goal: establish premium positioning and move the visitor toward an estimate request.

Sections:

- Hero
- Credibility
- Services preview
- Featured project
- Technology/material story
- Process
- Before/after
- Pricing preview
- Reviews
- Final CTA

### `/services`

Primary goal: help visitors understand service categories and select the relevant one.

Contains:

- service overview;
- comparison;
- expected outcomes;
- links to detailed service pages;
- CTA.

### `/services/ppf`

Primary goal: sell Paint Protection Film.

Contains:

- explanation;
- protection zones;
- benefits;
- material details;
- packages;
- process;
- warranty;
- FAQ;
- CTA.

### `/services/ceramic-coating`

Primary goal: explain ceramic protection and drive assessment requests.

### `/services/paint-correction`

Primary goal: explain defect removal, expected results, stages, and packages.

### `/services/interior-detailing`

Primary goal: show premium interior restoration and care.

### `/projects`

Primary goal: demonstrate proof of work.

Contains:

- grid/list of vehicles;
- filters by service if useful;
- strong photography;
- project metadata.

### `/projects/[slug]`

Primary goal: tell a detailed visual case study.

Contains:

- hero;
- vehicle info;
- challenge;
- services performed;
- process;
- gallery;
- before/after;
- technical data;
- result;
- CTA.

### `/pricing`

Primary goal: reduce uncertainty around cost.

Contains:

- starting prices;
- package logic;
- factors affecting final price;
- CTA to request exact estimate.

### `/about`

Primary goal: establish credibility and craftsmanship.

Contains:

- studio philosophy;
- process;
- environment;
- quality control;
- materials;
- certifications.

### `/contacts`

Primary goal: make contact frictionless.

Contains:

- phone;
- Telegram;
- WhatsApp;
- address;
- map;
- hours;
- short form;
- parking / arrival note.
